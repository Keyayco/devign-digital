About Devign

An independent studio, not an agency pretending to be bigger than it is.

Devign Digital designs and builds websites for independent businesses in Johannesburg.

It's a small, focused operation — the person who talks to you about your business is the same person who designs and builds the site. There's no handoff between a sales team and a delivery team, because there isn't one.

That's a deliberate choice. Working directly with each business means the site reflects what that business actually does, rather than a template dressed up to look custom.

The businesses Devign works with are usually first-time website owners: cleaning companies, events hosts, tradespeople and local service businesses that need a site that works, not a portfolio piece.

Every site so far is public and linked from the work page — nothing shown there is a mockup.

Have a business that needs a better website?

Tell us what you're building. We'll take it from there.
