Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what happens to information you provide through this website. It applies only to devigndigital.co.za.

What this site collects

The contact form on this site asks for your name, business name, email address, project type and message. Submitting the form opens your own email application with that information pre-filled, addressed to Devign Digital — the message is sent from your email account, not stored by this website.

If you email hello@devigndigital.co.za directly, that message and any information in it is received and stored in the same way as any other email, for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and, if a project goes ahead, for the length of that working relationship.

What this site does not do

This site does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party advertising scripts. It does not sell, share, or otherwise pass on any information you provide to any other party.

Embedded previews

The Work page embeds live previews of other websites Devign has built, hosted on their own domains (GitHub Pages, Netlify). Visiting this site loads those previews in the same way a browser tab would; those third-party sites are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Your information

You can ask what information Devign holds about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing hello@devigndigital.co.za.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects changes, this page will be updated to reflect that.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@devigndigital.co.za.
